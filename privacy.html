# NEXUS-AI — Privacy Policy **Effective date:** November 24, 2026 ## 1. Introduction NEXUS-AI is an open-source AI assistant for research, automation, intelligence gathering and content creation. This Privacy Policy explains how NEXUS-AI handles information when users operate the software and connect third-party services. ## 2. Information We May Process Depending on the features users enable, NEXUS-AI may process: - Account identifiers and profile information provided by connected platforms. - OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens. - User-created content, drafts and publishing instructions. - Platform video information and engagement data available through authorized APIs. - Configuration settings and information required to operate integrations. NEXUS-AI does not require users to provide information unrelated to the features they choose to use. ## 3. TikTok Integration When a user connects a TikTok account, NEXUS-AI may use authorized TikTok APIs to: - Authenticate the user's account. - Retrieve permitted basic profile information. - Retrieve video-list information when authorized. - Support additional features only when the relevant permissions have been approved and enabled. NEXUS-AI accesses only the information permitted by the scopes granted by the user and the permissions approved for the application. Users can revoke access through TikTok's account or application settings. ## 4. Credential Storage and Security NEXUS-AI is designed to store supported OAuth credentials locally using Windows Credential Manager. Credentials should not be shared publicly or committed to source-code repositories. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect credentials and integration data. However, no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. ## 5. How Information Is Used Information processed by NEXUS-AI may be used to: - Authenticate connected accounts. - Provide requested AI and automation features. - Retrieve authorized platform information. - Prepare content drafts. - Support user-approved publishing workflows. - Troubleshoot integration problems and improve software functionality. NEXUS-AI does not claim ownership of users' social media accounts or original content. ## 6. Sharing and Third-Party Services NEXUS-AI may communicate with third-party services when users activate integrations. These services may include TikTok, AI providers, research sources and other connected APIs. Information sent to a third-party service is subject to that service's own privacy policy and terms. NEXUS-AI does not sell users' personal information. ## 7. Publishing and User Approval Where publishing functionality is available, users remain responsible for the content they authorize. Human approval is required for publishing actions in the applicable workflows. Users can choose whether to connect accounts and enable supported integrations. ## 8. Data Retention and Deletion OAuth credentials may remain stored locally until users disconnect an integration or delete the credentials. Locally generated drafts, configuration files and other project data may remain on the user's device until deleted. Users are responsible for managing local project files and can revoke third-party access through the relevant platform. ## 9. Children's Privacy NEXUS-AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or platform age requirements. ## 10. International Data Transfers When users connect third-party services, information may be processed by those services in countries outside the user's country of residence. Such processing is governed by the relevant third-party service policies and applicable laws. ## 11. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as NEXUS-AI develops. The latest version will be published on this website with its effective date. ## 12. Contact For privacy questions, requests or concerns, users may contact the NEXUS-AI project through its official GitHub repository: https://github.com/Sarah-everything2/NEXUS-AI
